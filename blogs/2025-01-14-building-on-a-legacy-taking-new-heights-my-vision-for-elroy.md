by: Andrew Clare, CEO of Elroy Air Aviation has always been my passion and inspiration. Born and raised in the “cradle of aviation” in Long Island, NY, I spent countless hours at airports, gazing up at the sky and memorizing the names of different aircraft. In high school, my fascination only grew — so much so that I embarked on an independent research project on supersonic aircraft, driven by a deep desire to understand and push the limits of technology.
