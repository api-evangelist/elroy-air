Chaparral C1 flying at our Byron Airport facility in 2024, Byron CA It was 2017 and the Marines had a logistics problem. It was my first of many visits to the Pentagon, and as I sat down with a USMC Captain, I noticed commercial drones and drone parts everywhere: perched on desks, hanging from the ceiling, and in the corners of the room. US troops were being killed by roadside bombs in Afghanistan, and all hands were on deck to find solutions to this lethal problem.
