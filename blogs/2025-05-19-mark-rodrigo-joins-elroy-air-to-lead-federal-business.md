Mark Rodrigo joins Elroy Air to lead Federal Business Development, bringing both military and private sector expertise Today we welcome Mark Rodrigo to the Elroy Air team! Mark, who served as a captain in the United States Air Force joins us to lead Federal Business Development. As an intelligence officer, Mark spent his active duty career focused on ISR operations leveraging a mix of crewed, uncrewed, and space-based assets across five continents.
